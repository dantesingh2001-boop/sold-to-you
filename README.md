# SOLD TO YOU

Web edition of a 15-page magazine on the influence of advertising on young people in India.

- Ten ad breakdowns, a survey of 126 students, a checklist of the seven tricks ads use
- Print issue: `sold-to-you.pdf`
- All photographs are CC0 / public domain. Illustrations, charts and cover art were drawn for this issue.

No advertisements. No sponsored pages. No product was sent for review.
